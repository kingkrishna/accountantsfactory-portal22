ITR-5 Return Filing

ITR-5 is an Income Tax Return form designed for entities other than companies, including partnerships, LLPs, AOPs, BOIs, cooperative societies, and others, to report their income and taxes to the Indian Income Tax Department. This form is not applicable for individual taxpayers, HUFs, and entities required to file returns using ITR-6 or ITR-7.

Who is Eligible to File ITR-5?


Entities eligible to file ITR-5 include:

1. Firms: Partnership firms, excluding sole proprietorships.
2. Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs): Entities formed under the LLP Act.
3. Body of Individuals (BOI): A group of individuals earning income collectively.
4. Association of Persons (AOP): Associations created for a common purpose earning income.
5. Estate of Deceased Individuals: Estates managing the income of a deceased taxpayer.
6. Artificial Juridical Person: As defined under Section 2(31)(vii) of the Income Tax Act.
7. Business Trusts and Investment Funds: Trusts like REITs and Infrastructure Investment Trusts.
8. Estate of Insolvent Individuals: Estates managing the income of insolvent individuals.
9. Cooperative Societies: Societies formed under the Cooperative Societies Act.
10. Local Authorities: Municipal bodies and similar entities.

    Due Dates for Filing ITR-5

    The deadline to file ITR-5 depends on the entity's requirements:

  • 1. Accounts Audited Under the Income Tax Act:
    • Due date: 31st October of the assessment year.
  • 2. Filing Form 3CEB (for international/transfer pricing reports):
    • Due date: 30th November of the assessment year.
  • 3. Other Cases (No Audit Required):
    • Due date: 31st July of the assessment year.

    Who Cannot File ITR-5?

    The ITR 5 form is not meant for the following categories of taxpayers:

  • Individuals: They should use ITR-1, ITR-2, or other applicable forms.
  • Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs): HUFs use forms like ITR-2 or ITR-3.
  • Companies: Required to file using ITR-6.
  • Charitable Trusts, Political Parties, or Educational Institutions: These entities must use ITR-7 under Sections 139(4A), 139(4B), 139(4C), and 139(4D).
  • No Documents Required: It's important to note that there is no requirement to attach documents with the ITR 5 income tax form.


Penalties for Late Filing of ITR-5

  • 1. Late Filing Penalty Under Section 234F:
    • If the return is filed after the due date but before 31st December, a penalty of ₹5,000 will be imposed.
    • For taxpayers whose total income is less than ₹5 lakh, the penalty is reduced to ₹1,000.
  • 2. Interest Under Section 234A:
    • If there is any delay in paying the tax, interest will be charged at 1% per month or part of the month on the outstanding tax amount, from the due date of the tax until the date of actual payment.
  • 3. Penalty for Failure to File Return Under Section 271F:
    • A penalty of ₹5,000 may be imposed for not filing the ITR within the prescribed due date, even after the extended due date.


Documents Required

No physical documents are required to be attached with the ITR-5 form. However, taxpayers should maintain supporting records like:

  • Balance sheets.
  • Profit and loss statements.
  • Tax payment receipts.
  • Bank statements and invoices.




Structure of the ITR-5 Form

The ITR-5 Form is structured into several parts and schedules to facilitate the comprehensive reporting of income, deductions, and tax liability. Below is an overview of the structure of the ITR-5 Form:



Part A: General Information

Captures basic details like name, PAN, address, and nature of the business.

Part A-BS: Balance Sheet

Summarizes the entity's financial position at the end of the fiscal year.


Part A-Manufacturing Account

Provides details of manufacturing costs and outputs.


Part A-Trading Account

Contains details of trading activities for the fiscal year.


Part A-PL: Profit and Loss Account

Reports income and expenses for the financial year.


Part A-OI: Other Information

Includes additional financial details relevant to income computation.


Part A-QD: Quantitative Details

Quantitative data about goods produced, traded, or consumed.


Part B: Computation of Total Income

Calculates total taxable income.


Part B-TTI: Computation of Tax Liability

Determines tax liability based on total income.


Schedules

There are 31 schedules to comprehensively report income, deductions, and taxes, including:

  • Schedule HP: Income from house property.
  • Schedule BP: Business or professional income.
  • Schedule CG: Capital gains.
  • Schedule OS: Income from other sources.
  • Schedule VI-A: Deductions under Chapter VI-A.


Tax Payments Section

Details of advance tax, self-assessment tax, TDS (Tax Deducted at Source), and TCS (Tax Collected at Source).


Verification

Requires the taxpayer to sign and confirm the accuracy of the return.




Tax Payments:

This section includes details of payments made towards advance tax and self-assessment tax.
It also includes details of tax deducted at source on income other than salary (16A, 16B, 16C).
Additionally, it captures details of tax collected at the source.

Sequence for Filling the Form:
The Income Tax Department recommends the following order:

  • 1. Part A.
  • 2. Schedules.
  • 3. Part B-TI (Total Income).
  • 4. Part B-TTI (Tax Liability).
  • 5. Verification.

Filing Modes:
The ITR-5 form can be filed:

  • 1. Online: Through the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal.
    • Verification is done using Aadhaar OTP, EVC, or by sending a signed ITR-V to CPC Bengaluru.
  • 2. Offline: The offline method of filing ITR-5 involves downloading the relevant utility software (Excel or Java) from the Income Tax Department's official website. Taxpayers can fill in their return details offline, generate a JSON file, and upload it to the e-filing portal. Once uploaded, the return can be verified through Aadhaar OTP, EVC, or by sending a signed ITR-V to CPC Bengaluru.
