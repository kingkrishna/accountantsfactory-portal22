Current Regimes at a Glance (AY 2026-27)

Old Tax Regime

Best if you have > ₹3.5 Lakhs in deductions.

  • •Standard Deduction: ₹50,000
  • •Tax-Free Limit: ₹5 Lakh (u/s 87A)
  • •Allows HRA, LTA, 80C, 80D, Home Loan interest.

New Tax Regime

Default & Simple. Lower rates, fewer deductions.

  • •Standard Deduction: ₹75,000
  • •Tax-Free Limit: ₹12 Lakh (u/s 87A)
  • •Includes Marginal Relief for income > ₹12L.
