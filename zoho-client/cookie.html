Cookies Policy







This Cookies Policy explains how Accountants Factory LLP uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience when visiting our website.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and improve our services
  • Personalize your experience
2. Types of Cookies Used
  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the website to function and cannot be turned off.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website, so we can improve performance.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings for a more personalized experience.
  • Targeting/Advertising Cookies: Track browsing activities to provide relevant advertisements to you.
3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect your ability to use the full functionality of our website.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may use third-party services that set cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those third-party providers.

5. Changes to Cookie Policy

We reserve the right to change this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted here with an updated date.
