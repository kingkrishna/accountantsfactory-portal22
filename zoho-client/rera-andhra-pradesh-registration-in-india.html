Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) in Andhra Pradesh

The Andhra Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority (AP RERA) was established under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 to regulate the real estate sector and introduce transparency, accountability, and efficiency in real estate transactions. The State Government has enacted the Andhra Pradesh Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2017, which outline the implementation framework. Objectives of AP RERA


  • Consumer Protection:
    • Ensure that homebuyers are safeguarded against delays, fraud, and misrepresentation by developers and real estate agents.
    • Provide a legal platform for grievance redressal.
  • Transparency and Accountability:
    • Mandate the disclosure of vital project details, including approvals, development timelines, and financial status, on the AP RERA website.
    • Prevent false advertising and incomplete information in the real estate sector.
  • Efficient Dispute Resolution:
    • Offer a structured and quick resolution process for disputes through the RERA authority and the Appellate Tribunal.
  • Boost Sector Credibility:
    • Promote ethical practices among builders, developers, and agents, enhancing investor confidence.

Key Features of AP RERA


  • Mandatory Registration for Projects and Agents:
    • All real estate projects exceeding 500 square meters or involving more than eight apartments must register with RERA before advertising or selling units.
    • Real estate agents facilitating these transactions must also register.
  • Online Portal for Transparency:
    • The official AP RERA website serves as a repository for all project-related information, enabling buyers to access:
      • Approved layout and development plans.
      • Title deeds and land encumbrance certificates.
      • Financial disclosures and promoter details.
      • Completion schedules and milestones.
  • Strict Compliance Obligations:
    • Developers must deposit 70% of project funds in a separate escrow account to prevent fund diversion.
    • Non-compliance can lead to penalties, revocation of registration, or imprisonment.
  • Grievance Redressal:
    • AP RERA addresses complaints from homebuyers, agents, or developers regarding delays, fraud, or any non-compliance with the act.
    • Appeals against RERA decisions can be taken to the Appellate Tribunal.
  • Compensation for Delays:
    • Developers are liable to compensate buyers for project delays, as stipulated in the agreement.

Registration Process with AP RERA

For Developers:

1. Required Documents:

  • Details of the promoter (PAN, Aadhaar, photographs, address proof).
  • Financial statements, including the profit and loss statement, balance sheet, and ITR.
  • Project approvals, layout plans, title deeds, and encumbrance certificates.
  • Structural safety certificates and estimated completion timelines.

2. Fee Structure:

  • Registration fees are based on the project type (residential or commercial) and area size.
  • Fees can be calculated using the Registration Fee Calculator on the RERA portal.

3. Submission Process:

  • Applications can be submitted online via the AP RERA portal.
  • Fees must be paid via NEFT/RTGS.

For Agents:

1. Required Documents:

  • Identification documents (PAN, Aadhaar).
  • Business details and address proof.
  • Information about any ongoing legal disputes.

2. Fee Structure:

  • A fixed fee applies, varying for individuals and companies.

Complaint Registration Process

Buyers, developers, or agents can file complaints regarding non-compliance, delays, or fraudulent practices.

  • Visit the AP RERA portal at www.rera.ap.gov.in.
  • Navigate to the 'Registration' menu and select 'Complaint Registration.'
  • Fill out the complaint form, specifying:
    • Details of the complainant and respondent.
    • Nature of the complaint.
    • Supporting documents, if applicable.
  • Submit the form after payment of the complaint filing fee.

Penalties for Non-Compliance

Developers:

  • Fines up to 10% of the project cost for failure to register.
  • Imprisonment or higher fines for repeated offenses.
Agents:
  • Penalties for facilitating transactions without registration.
Homebuyers:
  • Buyers providing false complaints or misleading information can also face penalties.


Benefits of RERA in Andhra Pradesh

  • For Homebuyers:
    • Enhanced protection against project delays, fraud, and misrepresentation.
    • A transparent process to assess a project’s progress and legality.
  • For Developers:
    • Increased credibility and trust among customers and investors.
    • Standardized practices and reduced litigation risks.
  • For Real Estate Agents:
    • Registration enhances their professional credibility.
    • Legal framework to operate within ethical boundaries.
