Frequently Asked Questions


Are your Zoho certifications publicly verifiable?

Yes. Nitin Kumar Doranadula is listed as a Personal Trainer and Vision Connects Foundation as a Training Institute on zoho.com/in/books/zohobooks-certification/. Accountants Factory LLP is a Zoho Authorized Partner — verifiable at zoho.com/in/books/accountant/find-accountant.

What certificate do I get after completing training?

Institute programs lead to a Zoho Prime Student Certificate. One-on-one virtual training leads to an official Associate Certificate. Both are issued directly by Zoho, not by us.

Can you migrate our existing Tally / QuickBooks data?

Yes — we handle masters (customers, vendors, items), opening balances and multi-year historical transactions. Data is reconciled and audited before go-live so nothing is lost.

Do you offer GST e-invoicing and e-way bill automation?

Yes — fully configured within Zoho Books with IRN generation, e-way bill linkage and GSTR-1/3B reconciliation. Compliance dashboards give you real-time status.

Is training available in Telugu?

Yes. One-on-one virtual training and selected corporate batches are delivered in Telugu or English — whichever helps your team adopt faster.
